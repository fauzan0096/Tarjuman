Open this video on YouTube

A web page cannot download YouTube audio, so use YouTube's own transcript, which is free:

  1. Open the video in the YouTube app or site.
  2. Tap the description (the "…more" part).
  3. Scroll down and tap Show transcript.
  4. Tap the ⋮ menu and turn timestamps off if you see it. Select all the text and copy it.
  5. Come back here and tap Paste copied transcript (or paste into the box below).

This works when the video has captions, including auto-generated ones. If "Show transcript" is missing, the video has none. Then download the audio yourself with the owner's permission and use the Recording tab.

Settings and limits

Translation uses the free MyMemory service. Anonymous use allows only a few thousand characters per day, so long transcripts may stop part way. Adding your email raises the limit a lot. For long texts, use "Better translation with Claude": it copies a ready prompt that you paste into the Claude app.
