Add your free Google key (accurate Urdu, photos, YouTube)

Without a key the app still works in a basic way. With a free Google key, speech, photos and YouTube become much more accurate.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key and copy the key.
  3. Paste it below and tap Save and test.

The key is kept only in this browser on this device. Never send it to anyone and never put it in a file.

On Google's free plan, what you send may be used by Google to improve its products. Do not use it for private recordings, photos or documents.
